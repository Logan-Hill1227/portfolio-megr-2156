## Reflection
In feature b, in order to find diameter I used the strength equation, putting these values into my parameters ensuring they would be factored in when I designed part b.

For my tolerances I chose to give A, B, C all tighter tolerances ensuring a snug fit within the bracket. I gave the overall height a looser tolerance as these dimesnions are non essential in comparison. In result, these tighter tolerances would slow production down and increase the cost of production.

In all, this assignment took me around 4 hours to complete.

## Lessons Learned
One of the most valuable lessons I gained from this is importance of proper communication in drawings. Without proper communication the manufactures can get confused on certain elements or even produce incorrect outputs. This backs up production and causes a loss of profit as well.

Secondly, tolerances are important in relation to each other especially in minimized area. Clearance should be taken into account as well to allow some movement.
