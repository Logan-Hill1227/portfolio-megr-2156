Parameters I was given include, the force on the shaft of the motor equalling 300 N and a required Safety Factor of 3. The maximum deformation allowed was 0.30 mm. 
## Feature 1

As required my first step of action was to list my knowns, and unknowns. I hd the freedom to choose my material in which I chose PLA for the yield stregnth of modulus of elasticity.

I was given the ability to decide the length and base dimensions of the feature in which I chose 40mm and 35mm respectievely. I chose these dimensions to ensure the gearbox would have no problem fitting into the frame. Following this step I drew out a FBD showing locations and directions of forces for whihc I then used to solve my equations. When solving these equations I calculated two potential heights, one by deformation and one by stress. In this case I went with the stress height as the deformation height would essentially be wasting material when produced as the stress height is shorter in comparison but still meets the safety factor.

I extruded this object 2mm into my part for stablization. Using the drawing of the gearbox, I used the 22mm for the bolt holes to then place 4 3.4mm holes each 90 degrees apart around the 22mm circle (centered) fixed about the indent.
