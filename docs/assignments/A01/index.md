Product: Spring Clothespin
  a) The primary function of this product is to generate a clamping force between two jaws in order to hold and keep a certain article in place relative to the clothespin. As an engineering function this product uses the conversion of spring deformation into a compressive jaw force to accomplish its primary function. 
  
  b) 
  i)A model for this would be a torsion spring driving a lever. For the spring Ts​=(kθ)​θ where T is equal to the torque produced by the spring, k is the constant, and θ is the angular deflection. For the two jaws we can use the equation Ts=NL where N is the normal clmaping force and L is the effective moment arm.
  ii) One assumption that will make this model valid for this product is that the jaws and the spring would remain in elastic range and we can treat the two jaws as rigid bodies. This is a valid assumption because real wood clothespins have compliance withing the wood and plastics jaws, spring contact, and friction of the surfaces.
